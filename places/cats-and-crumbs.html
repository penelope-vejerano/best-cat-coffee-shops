MARIKINA CITY · BEST OVERALL

Cats & Crumbs

Cats & Crumbs is my top choice for a slow, meaningful afternoon. Its homemade comfort food and Sagada coffee make it easy to stay beyond one cup, while its foster cats and adoption mission give every visit a purpose beyond the café experience. I recommend it to visitors who want a warm meal, a relaxed atmosphere, and the chance to support cats as they wait for permanent homes.
