MEET YOUR GUIDE

Penelope Vejerano

I am a cat and coffee enthusiast who enjoys finding welcoming cafés where visitors can relax with a great drink while spending time with adorable cats.

I started Best Cat Coffee Shops because I wanted one cozy place where fellow cat lovers could discover cafés that care about good coffee, comfortable spaces, and the well-being of their resident cats.

Every recommendation is chosen for its atmosphere, cat-friendly experience, and the memorable connection it creates between people, cafés, and cats.
